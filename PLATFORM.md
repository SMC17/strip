# Strip Platform

Strip separates authoritative computation from proposals, visualizations, and
external integrations.

```text
product surfaces
  -> permissioned tools and AeroIR
  -> scenario / assurance composition
  -> deterministic physics and replay
  -> evidence, units, frames, validity, identity
```

## Product distributions

- Consumer Lab: simulation, education, modding, and autonomy research.
- Civil Assurance: requirements, hazards, scenarios, tests, and evidence.
- Mission Engineering: public-safe readiness, resilience, and generic
  system-of-systems studies.

These are interfaces over one substrate, not claims of operational approval.
Controlled data and controlled implementations require separate repositories,
deployments, access policy, and review.

## Authority order

1. accepted physical evidence for the declared context
2. validated numerical or analytical models
3. bounded reduced-order or learned models with discrepancy evidence
4. generative perception and language-model proposals

AI proposals enter candidate state. Typed validation, deterministic evaluation,
assurance dependencies, and human promotion determine whether they become an
authoritative project baseline.

## Current implementation boundary

The atmosphere, geodesy, math, dynamics, controls, sensors, scenario, UAS, and
assurance foundations execute locally. External adapters and frontier modules
are contracts until their implementations and evidence are linked explicitly.
